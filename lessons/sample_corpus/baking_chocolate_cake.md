# Chocolate Cake

A reliable plain chocolate cake that does not depend on a precise baking
chemistry trick.

## Ingredients structure

- 200g plain flour, sifted
- 75g cocoa powder
- 250g butter, softened
- 350g caster sugar
- 4 large eggs
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 200ml buttermilk
- 200g dark chocolate, melted and cooled

## Method

Cream the butter and sugar for a full five minutes. This step is what produces
the fine open crumb, and it is the one most often underdone.

Add the eggs one at a time, mixing fully between each. Fold in the dry
ingredients in three additions, alternating with the buttermilk. Add the
melted chocolate last, folding gently so you keep the air you just built up.

## Baking

175C, 35 minutes, in a lined 20cm tin. The cake is done when a skewer comes out
with a few moist crumbs rather than clean — an entirely clean skewer usually
means it will be dry.

## Storage

Keeps three days wrapped at room temperature. Freezes well for two months,
layered with baking paper.
